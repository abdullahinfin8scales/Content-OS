# 002 · Floating word-count pill and top-bar "Saved" indicator

- **Removed:** 2026-11-09
- **Requested by:** the agent, after reviewing screenshots

## What it was
A pill floating over the bottom-right of the document showing words, characters and spoken time, plus a "Saved" dot in the editor's top bar.

## Why it was removed
The pill covered the text being written, and splitting status across two places cluttered the top bar.

## What replaced it
One status bar under the editor: save state on the left, words, characters and spoken time on the right (counts switch to the selection when text is selected).

## Bringing it back
Don't float anything over document text (mistake 008).
